JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

July 25 – July 31, 2026 · Full List of Every Special Movement

This was an Airbus A380 heavy week at JFK, with Emirates, Korean Air, and Asiana all running multiple superjumbo rotations, including two separate Emirates NBA-livery appearances and a run of standard "Livery" special-scheme A380s throughout. The clear standout, though, was The Yucaipa Companies' Boeing 757-2J4(WL), tagged Very Rare + VVIP, arriving from Washington-Dulles on July 31st. Israir Airlines also made its inaugural JFK-Tel Aviv rotation on an Airbus A330, joining GOL Airlines' now-recurring new route to Rio. July 28th stood out as the busiest cargo day of the week, with Amazon Air, FedEx, UPS, and DHL all running back-to-back freighter rotations from dawn through midnight. Special liveries were everywhere, from Saudia and SAS's "80 Years" schemes to Uzbekistan Airways' retro Dreamliner, while an Atlas Air 747 repositioned to Naval Station Norfolk under a Military tag mid-week.
