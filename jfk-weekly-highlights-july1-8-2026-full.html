JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

July 1 – July 8, 2026 · Full List of Every Special Movement

This was a Fleet Week-heavy stretch dominated by Navy and Marine Corps rotary traffic, including repeated Air Force Two (C-32A), Marine Two (MV-22B Osprey), Navy C-37B, and an Extremely Rare C-17A Globemaster III. The Russian Special Flight Detachment's Ilyushin Il-96-300PU shuttled between JFK and Washington-Dulles multiple times across the week, and the Ukrainian Air Alliance An-12BK ran back-to-back Extremely Rare cargo rotations. Rounding things out were German Air Force and Alpha Star/K5 Aviation VIP charter jets, an Israir new-route arrival, a DHL diversion, and a steady stream of widebody freighters from SF Airlines, Cathay Cargo, Challenge Airlines, AirZeta, and Air Transport International.
