New USAF Boeing VC-25B Marks Historic Arrival at New York (JFK) as Air Force One

A historic milestone in presidential aviation took place at John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) as the United States Air Force's new Boeing VC-25B "Bridge", registration 25-33000, made its first appearance at the airport operating as Air Force One (AF1).

The aircraft arrived from Joint Base Andrews (ADW) at 3:08 PM EDT, touching down on Runway 31L before parking for a brief ground stop. Just over an hour later, the aircraft departed at 4:23 PM EDT, once again using Runway 31L for its return flight to Andrews.

The Boeing VC-25B represents the next generation of presidential airlift, replacing the aging VC-25A fleet that has served U.S. presidents for more than three decades. The aircraft features significantly upgraded communications, mission systems, defensive capabilities, and modernized interiors while retaining the iconic Air Force One livery recognized around the world.

Air Force One visits are among the rarest aircraft movements seen at JFK, and the arrival of the new VC-25B makes this event even more significant. Spotters gathered across the airport to witness one of the first operational appearances of the aircraft, making it one of the most notable aviation events of 2026 at JFK.

The debut of the VC-25B at JFK marks a historic chapter for both presidential aviation and the airport's long history of hosting high-profile government aircraft. As additional VC-25Bs enter service, appearances at JFK are expected to remain exceptionally uncommon, making this visit one of the most memorable aircraft movements ever documented by AvHouse JFK.
