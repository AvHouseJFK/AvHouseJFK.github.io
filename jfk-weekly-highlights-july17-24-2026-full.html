JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

July 17 – July 24, 2026 · Full List of Every Special Movement

The undisputed highlight of the week was Air Force One itself — the Boeing VC-25B touched down and departed JFK on July 17th, tagged Extremely Rare, alongside a formation of Marine Corps MV-22B Ospreys and a C-17A Globemaster III. World Cup-related traffic from Aerolíneas Argentinas ran heavy all week, with multiple Very Rare A330 rotations to and from Buenos Aires. Government and state aircraft were well represented too, including a Spanish Air Force A310 and Falcon 900 shuttling diplomats, a Nigerian Air Force Global 7500, and an Angolan Government-chartered Global 7500 via Eden Jets. Delta's Boeing 717 made an Extremely Rare appearance on a JFK-Chicago hop, American Airlines logged a same-flight-number diversion back into JFK, and special liveries turned up on Emirates' "Dest. Dubai" A380 and Gulf Air's retro-scheme 787. Cargo and freighter traffic remained the backbone of the week, with Atlas Air, Cathay Cargo, Challenge Airlines, Saudia Cargo, and AirZeta all logging multiple widebody rotations.
