JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

July 11 – July 16, 2026 · Full List of Every Special Movement

The week opened with a pair of United Arab Emirates Air Force Airbus A330-243 MRTT tankers, a Rare + Military combo rarely seen on the civil side of JFK, followed by a Union Aviation Embraer Lineage 1000 VIP arrival. Wednesday brought heavy widebody cargo and passenger traffic from Cathay Cargo, Challenge Airlines, Hawaiian, National, and Emirates, plus a GOL Airlines Airbus A330 marking a New Airline entry from Rio. Thursday's Hyundai 737 BBJ corporate shuttle and a formation of Marine Corps MV-22B Ospreys added variety, while Friday delivered two special-livery A380s — Emirates' NBA scheme and Korean Air's standard A388 — alongside a Sunwing-liveried WestJet 737 MAX and a Vista America Global 7500. The week closed with a busy Globemaster III day, as Air Force C-17As rotated through Andrews multiple times, bookended by CommuteAir Embraer E170 regional hops and a KaiserAir charter.
