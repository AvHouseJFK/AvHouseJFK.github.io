GOL Linhas Aéreas Launches Historic Nonstop Service Connecting Rio de Janeiro and New York (JFK)

By AvHouse News Desk • Published July 30, 2026

Brazilian carrier GOL Linhas Aéreas officially launched its non-stop long-haul expansion connecting Rio de Janeiro’s Galeão International Airport (GIG) directly to New York’s John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK).

Covering a distance of approximately 4,768 miles, this flagship route marks GOL's first venture into widebody long-haul operations. To bridge the nearly 10-hour trans-equatorial gap—which far exceeds the range of single-aisle narrowbody jets—GOL has partnered to wet-lease widebody Airbus A330-200 equipment (operated in partnership with Wamos Air) to initiate 3x weekly service into JFK.

A Major Expansion for Rio & New York Operations

The nonstop corridor opens up critical capacity for passenger and belly-hold cargo traffic between South America and North America. By channeling passengers through its principal hub at Rio's Galeão Airport, GOL offers easy connections to secondary Brazilian markets while feeding its partner networks in the United States.

"Launching direct service between Rio de Janeiro and New York JFK represents a milestone chapter in GOL's international routing plan, unlocking direct access for international business and leisure travel between two global powerhouses."

Official Flight Schedule (3x Weekly)

The 4,768-mile trans-equatorial flight features an overnight schedule in both directions for maximum convenience and optimal connecting windows.

Service & Cabin Specifications

The dual-class widebody Airbus A330-200 provides the range and capacity required for the 10-hour flight, featuring lie-flat business seats alongside an expanded economy cabin to accommodate high seasonal demand.

Route Key Facts:

  • Stage Length: 4,768 miles (~10 hours block time)
  • Service Frequency: 3 flights per week in each direction
  • JFK Operations: Handled through JFK's international terminal facilities
  • Strategic Value: Establishes a direct long-haul presence for GOL in the U.S. Northeast
