JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

August 17 – August 24, 2026 · Special & Notable Movements Only

The week's headline story was a Qatari head-of-state visit: the Amiri Flight Airbus A340-211 arrived from Denver on the 22nd and didn't lift off again for Milan until the following night, tagged Government and Extremely Rare both ways. The French Air Force sent a CASA CN-235 tactical transport through on a same-day turn on the 18th, the rarest single-day showing of the week. Business aviation stayed active with Qatar Executive's Gulfstream G650ER bouncing through JFK twice, a Very Rare Global Jet Luxembourg BBJ3, a HondaJet on a rare domestic hop, and an Epic E1000 single-engine turboprop. Special liveries turned up on Emirates' "Dest. Dubai" A380 (twice), a Star Alliance-liveried Lufthansa A340, and a fresh Cargolux 747-8 scheme. Operational oddities included a Lufthansa Cargo 777 diverting off an ORD-FRA routing and a rare departure runway assignment for a Cathay Cargo 747. General aviation seaplane traffic showed up via Cessna Caravans on both the 19th and 23rd.
