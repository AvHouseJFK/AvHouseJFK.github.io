July 2026 Monthly Flight Highlights at JFK

Covering July 1 – July 31, 2026 · Compiled from daily spotting logs

July was a stacked month at JFK, headlined by Fleet Week military traffic, a Presidential visit, two new airline launches, a steady rotation of Airbus A380 superjumbos, and a run of genuinely rare cargo and government aircraft. Below is a roundup of the month's biggest movements, followed by the full day-by-day log.

🇺🇸 Presidential & Government Traffic

The month's single biggest catch was Air Force One — a Boeing VC-25B (25-33000) — arriving July 17 from Joint Base Andrews and departing the same evening, escorted on the ground by Marine Corps MV-22 Ospreys and supported by a USAF C-17A Globemaster III. Fleet Week (July 2–4 and July 14) brought a steady stream of US Navy MH-60S Knighthawks and Marine Corps Ospreys shuttling between JFK and the Wall Street Heliport (JRB), alongside Air Force Two (a Boeing C-32A) on July 4.

Foreign state aircraft were also well represented: a German Air Force Airbus A319 ACJ passed through twice (July 3 and July 5), two UAE Air Force A330 MRTT tankers visited July 11, Russia's Special Flight Detachment flew an Ilyushin IL-96-300PU through JFK on three separate occasions (July 2, 6, and 8), a Nigerian Air Force Global 7500 arrived July 18, and Spain's Ministry of Defense rotated an Airbus A310 and Dassault Falcon 900 through JFK July 18–20. An Angolan government Global 7500 rounded out the month's diplomatic traffic on July 23.

✈️ New Airlines at JFK

  • GOL Linhas Aéreas — Airbus A330-243 (EC-NBN)
    Began regular JFK–Rio de Janeiro (GIG) service in July, with rotations logged July 13, 21, 27, and 31.
  • Israir Airlines — Airbus A330-243 (4X-BAK)
    Flew its inaugural JFK service from Tel Aviv (TLV) on July 31, arriving 5:54 PM EDT.

🐘 A380 Superjumbo Rotation

Emirates, Korean Air, and Asiana Airlines kept the Airbus A380 in near-daily rotation through JFK all month, mostly on the Dubai (DXB), Seoul (ICN), and Milan (MXP) routes. A few stood out for their liveries: Emirates flew an NBA-branded A380 on July 15 and again July 27, and closed the month with an A380 wearing a "Destination Dubai" scheme on July 23.
