JFK Weekly Flight Highlights

August 1 – August 8, 2026 · Rare, Government, VIP, Livery & General Aviation Movements Only

The week's standout was the Qatar Amiri Flight A320 ACJ Prestige, which made two separate Government + Rare rotations through JFK on both August 1st and August 2nd. Israir Airlines launched brand-new JFK–Tel Aviv service on August 1st, tagged New Airline + Inaugural. General aviation rarities were everywhere, headlined by an FAA Beech King Air 300 (Very Rare) on August 5th, plus a Beech 36 Bonanza, a Cirrus G2 Vision Jet, and a Socata TBM-700 all logging Rare appearances. Business jet traffic included a Rare Jet Aviation BBJ and a Rare Gulfstream IV, while Delta's Airbus A330 arrived from Athens wearing a Team USA livery. Cargolux's Boeing 747 turned up in a special "Car50lux" 50th-anniversary scheme on August 3rd, and Emirates ran both its Skywards and Dest. Dubai special liveries during the week. Two irregular operations closed out the period on August 8th: an Air France A330 diverted from EWR (Diversion + Rare) and a Cargolux 747 that declared an emergency diversion inbound from Luxembourg.
